# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- The app has a new look: cream, paprika and basil colours, round buttons and a new font. It also works offline and in the dark theme.
- **Explore** replaces the notes: add your restaurants with their cuisine, area, price (€ to €€€€), your rating and a note. Each place is a card with its star dishes and a heart for your favourites. Search by name, area, cuisine or dish, with or without accents. Tap a card to see the place; slide a card to the left to delete it, with **Undo**. The **Roulette** has its section already and comes soon.
- Add the dishes of each place with **Add dish**: their price, your rating, a note and whether it is a star dish. Star dishes come first. Tap a dish to change or delete it, with **Undo**.
- Save where each place is: **Use my position**, or paste a Google Maps link or "lat, lng". **Route** opens the directions in Google Maps.
- Filter the places with the buttons under the search: **Price** (€ to €€€€), **Distance** (less than 1, 2, 5 or 10 km from you), **Favourites** and **Cuisine**. With your position, each card shows how far it is and the nearest places come first. Your filters stay on the phone. **Can't decide?** takes you to the roulette.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-09

- First version of Spinach. It works offline, installs on Android and has light and dark themes in English and Spanish. Sign in with Google, or use it with no account. **Delete all data** in Settings starts the app as new. Your restaurants, dishes and roulette come next.
