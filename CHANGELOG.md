# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first.

## Unreleased

- The Warm Gourmet look of `DESIGN.md` (DSV-5): cream, paprika and basil tokens with a light and a dark value each, `--wheel-1` to `--wheel-4`, 24px cards, capsule buttons and the font Plus Jakarta Sans, self-hosted in `fonts/` (SIL OFL) and cached in `SHELL`. `--primary` is the dark paprika `#b33f00`, so white text on it reaches 5.8:1.
- Explore replaces the notes demo (PLC-20 to PLC-27): `src/ui/explore.js` (cards, search through `visiblePlaces`, empty state, swipe to delete) and `src/ui/place.js` (detail in the `pane`, the place sheet, favourite, delete with Undo, `openPlace`). Sections are `explore`, `roulette` (a stub) and `settings`; `roulette` is the one module (NAV-1, MOD-1). The doc is `pwa-foodie.places`; Export and Import carry it. The drag module (`src/ui/drag.js`, `src/reorder.js`) is gone. New icons `compass`, `wheel`, `heart`, `star`; tile tokens `--tile-1` to `--tile-4`.
- Dishes and the location of a place (PLC-24, PLC-28 to PLC-31, LOC-7, LOC-8): the detail lists the dishes with rating and note and edits them in `#dish-sheet` (`addDish`, `editDish`, `deleteDish` with Undo, the price read by `parsePrice`); the place sheet has a location field (Use my position, a pasted map link read by `parseLocation`, Clear) and the detail a **Route** link (`routeUrl`, new tab) and the distance from a fresh fix. New `src/ui/position.js` (`readPosition`, `currentFix`, `silentFix`; the fix lives in `ui/context.js`, `setFix`). `ui.sheetOpen` is now `ui.sheet` (`place`, `dish` or null), so Escape closes the open sheet. `src/ui/motion.js` `collapse` closes a deleted card or dish row. New icons `route` and `pin`.
- Filter chips and distance in Explore (PLC-20, PLC-21, PLC-32 to PLC-36, LOC-9, LOC-10): new `src/ui/filters.js` (`renderChips(container, { mode })` with Price, Distance, Favourites, Cuisine and, in `dishes` mode for the roulette, Max price; `#filter-sheet`, a third named sheet `ui.sheet = 'filter'`). The filters live in `ui/context.js` (`filters`, `setFilters`, saved under `pwa-foodie.filters`). A distance pick reads the position; a failure shows on the chip, a saved distance with no fix waits and filters nothing. Explore calls `silentFix` when it comes into view, shows the distance on the cards and sorts nearest first. The "Can't decide?" card goes to Roulette and hides with the module off. A horizontal drag on the chip row no longer changes the section.
- The Roulette section (RUL-10 to RUL-15): new `src/ui/roulette.js` (`bind`, `renderRoulette`) replaces the stub and its `roulette.soon` text. A **Places** / **Dishes** switch (`ui.mode`), the shared filter chips (`renderChips`, Max price in Dishes mode), and a wheel of up to 12 segments drawn as a CSS conic gradient with a label and an emoji on each segment, "12 of N" above it. **Spin** turns the disc to the winner of `drawWheel` by `landingAngle` over `SPIN_MS` (`ui.spinning` disables the controls); reduced motion shows the result at once. The result card has **Let's go** (`routeUrl`, disabled with no location), **Open** (`openPlace`) and **Spin again** (`exclude: ui.lastWinner`); a change of mode or filters clears the last winner. 0 candidates show an empty state that goes to Explore, 1 shows the result at once. New tokens `--on-wheel-1` to `--on-wheel-4` keep the segment text at 4.5:1 or more, plus `--wheel-size`, `--roulette-max` and `--spin-ease`. The Roulette calls `silentFix` when it comes into view.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-09

- First release, from pwa-template 0.1.4: offline shell, install, update toast, What's new, Google or offline account, English and Spanish, light and dark themes, Delete all data. The notes demo stands in for the restaurants, dishes and roulette. The release notes start with this version; the template's 0.1.2 to 0.1.4 entries, which this app never shipped, go. `DESIGN.md` holds the design system of the Stitch project "Social Food Roulette PWA" (projects/6761532087147787488), the target design.
- The app runs on `https://spinach.artisandevs.duckdns.org/`, proxied by pwa-server from the Pages site, as Bodega and Attire do, and calls the server at `https://api.artisandevs.duckdns.org` (`PRODUCTION_API`, ACC-3).
